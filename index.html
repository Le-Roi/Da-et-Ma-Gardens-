OUR STORY · MORE THAN 30 YEARS Rooted in Family, Grown with Purpose Da et Ma Gardens grew from the family farming journey of Daniel and Margaret. They began farming to provide milk for their young family. When neighbours started buying surplus milk, a family activity began developing into an agricultural enterprise. Today, we bring together dairy farming, poultry and beekeeping. We remain committed to serving our community, handling products carefully and building our farm responsibly. OUR FARMING APPROACH Care, Hygiene and Responsibility We place importance on careful handling, hygiene, responsible care for our animals and bees, and honest communication with customers. We aim to minimise unnecessary chemical use where practical and continue improving our farm management and record-keeping practices. We do not claim organic certification. Customers should enquire about the handling, storage and requirements relevant to individual products.

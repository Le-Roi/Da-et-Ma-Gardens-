MORE THAN 30 YEARS OF FARMING

A family farm with a story worth sharing.

Da et Ma Gardens began with Daniel and Margret, who farmed to provide milk for their young family. As neighbours began purchasing their surplus milk, a family tradition grew into a business.

Today, we bring together dairy farming, poultry and beekeeping, guided by a commitment to responsible production, hygiene and care for our customers.
